Privacy policy

Updated November 9, 2026

Data stored by Rumbo

Your account records, balances, expenses, income, categories, notes, budgets, goals, recurring payments and preferences are stored on your device first. My accounts organizes payment methods; it does not connect real bank accounts. If you create an account, Supabase processes your email, user identifier, name and country for authentication and stores a copy of that data to synchronize it across your devices. Data is transmitted over HTTPS, and database access rules restrict each user to their own copy.

Optional voice dictation

Your phone’s speech recognition service accesses the microphone when you start dictation. Depending on its provider and settings, it may send audio to its servers for transcription under its own policies. Rumbo receives text and stores a local draft until you confirm or discard it; it does not retain audio files. Confirmed text may remain in the transaction note. You can enter transactions manually without enabling the microphone.

Optional Gmail import

Beta 0.3.32 ships with this feature disabled. In a version where it is enabled, Rumbo will request read-only Gmail access only after you activate the feature and accept the prior disclosure. Processing will occur on your phone. Rumbo will not modify or delete email, store full message content or temporary access tokens, or synchronize them with Supabase. Only transaction details you review and confirm and a one-way message fingerprint used to prevent duplicates will be saved. If you use a Rumbo account, the confirmed transaction may be included in your synchronized copy like any manually entered transaction. You will be able to disconnect Gmail in Settings to revoke access.

Notifications

Limits and due date reminders are calculated from records on your device. Notifications are local and require your permission. We do not send amounts to an external notification service. Sound and visibility depend on Android settings.

Backups and third parties

Supabase provides authentication and synchronization; Rumbo’s project is hosted in the United States. Its policy is available at Supabase Privacy Policy. Exported JSON files contain financial records, and you choose where to store them. Android automatic backup is disabled. Beta 0.3.32 ships with ads disabled but includes the AdMob SDK. If ads are enabled in a future version, Google’s SDK may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Rumbo does not send transactions, notes, balances or dictation to AdMob. Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and Google partner technologies. The phone’s speech service may require Internet access.

Sale, advertising and data use

Rumbo does not sell personal or financial data. Financial records and Gmail information—if that feature is ever enabled—are not transferred to advertising platforms, data brokers or credit providers and are not used for advertising, creditworthiness, lending or training general-purpose artificial intelligence models. Any future use of information received from Google APIs must comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Without an account, records remain only on the phone until you delete them. With an account, the Supabase copy is kept while the account is active. Deleting the account from Your profile also deletes its synchronized copy and clears local data on that phone. Exported files and local copies on other devices must be deleted separately. Unconfirmed voice drafts are not synchronized.

Your control

You can view and export records, manage accounts and delete transactions. You can use Rumbo without signing in, retry synchronization, sign out or permanently delete your account from Your profile. You can also start a request outside the app at Account deletion page. Protect access to your email and phone. Exported JSON files have no additional encryption.

Changes

This policy describes the current beta. If processed data, providers, payments or advertising change, the policy and related notices will be updated.

Privacy policy

Updated September 27, 2026

Data stored by Rumbo

Your account records, balances, expenses, income, categories, notes and preferences are stored on your device. This beta does not create a user account or synchronize records with our servers. My accounts organizes payment methods; it does not connect real bank accounts.

Optional voice dictation

Your phone’s speech recognition service accesses the microphone when you start dictation. Depending on its provider and settings, it may send audio to its servers for transcription under its own policies. Rumbo receives text and stores a local draft until you confirm or discard it; it does not retain audio files. Confirmed text may remain in the transaction note. You can enter transactions manually without enabling the microphone.

Notifications

Limits are calculated from expenses recorded on your device. Notifications are local and require your permission. We do not send amounts to an external notification service. Sound and visibility depend on Android settings.

Backups and third parties

Exported JSON files contain financial records. You choose where to store and share them. Do not publish them. Android automatic backup is disabled in this version. The current production flow does not include advertising or usage analytics. The phone’s speech service may require Internet access.

Retention and deletion

Records remain locally until you delete them. In Settings → Your data you can export a backup or delete transactions and reset accounts and categories. This does not delete exported copies or all preferences. Close and confirm to discard a voice draft. To erase all local data, including preferences and drafts, use Android App info → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall Rumbo. Delete exported files separately.

Your control

You can view and export records, manage accounts and delete transactions. We do not hold a remote copy to restore for you. Protect access to your phone and keep backups private. Exported files are not promised additional encryption.

Changes

This policy describes the current beta. Before adding online accounts, synchronization, payments, advertising or other providers, the policy and notices must be updated.

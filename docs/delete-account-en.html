Delete your Rumbo account

In the app, open Settings → Your profile → Delete account. Deletion is permanent.

If you cannot access the app, email us from the address associated with your account.

Request deletion by email

Data deleted

Your authentication account and synchronized wallets, transactions, preferences, budgets, goals, recurring payments and loans stored with Supabase.

Data that may remain

Exported files and local copies on other phones must be deleted separately. Rumbo does not intentionally retain another copy of your records after account deletion; providers may temporarily retain technical logs or backups where required for legal or security purposes.
